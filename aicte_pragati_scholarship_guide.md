# Finding Scholarship Support After Class 12

Paying for college can be difficult when you are still comparing courses, colleges, and entrance exams. Scholarships can help, but the information is often spread across different pages. You may need to check the scholarship name, who can apply, how much support is available, and the final date for submitting an application.

The **AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls** is an opportunity for students planning their next step after 12th. It is offered by the All India Council for Technical Education, also known as AICTE. The scholarship information brings the main details together so you can review them before applying.

Do not rely only on the scholarship name. Eligibility rules matter, and an application can be missed if you do not check the requirements carefully. Keep your course and college information ready, then compare it with the conditions given on the scholarship page.

## Understanding the AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls

The scholarship provides ₹50,000 per year along with a ₹2,000 per month stipend, according to the available details. This support may be useful when you are planning education costs after 12th and looking at eligible study options.

The scholarship is specifically for girls, but that alone does not confirm that every student can apply. Read the eligibility section in full before starting the application. Check the course details, institution requirements, and any documents or conditions mentioned for applicants.

The deadline shown for this scholarship is **December 31, 2026**. A deadline gives you a target, but it is better not to leave the application until the final day. You may need time to collect documents, review your information, or resolve an issue with the application process.

## How to Use the Scholarship Information

Start by reading the complete eligibility information on the [AICTE Pragati Scholarship India page](https://collegeafter12.com/scholarships/aicte-pragati-scholarship-girls). Note the conditions that apply to you and make a simple list of anything you need before applying.

It also helps to keep scholarship research alongside your college search. While comparing institutions, look at the course, location, admission route, and available financial support. A scholarship can be useful, but it should be considered as part of the wider decision rather than as the only reason to choose a college.
