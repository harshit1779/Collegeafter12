# A Practical Guide to Finding a Minority Merit Scholarship After 12th

Choosing a college after 12th often means managing several decisions at once. You need to compare colleges, understand courses, track exams, and look for ways to manage education costs. Scholarships can be an important part of that process, especially when the application has a clear deadline and specific eligibility conditions.

The **Merit-cum-Means Scholarship for Minorities** is provided by the Ministry of Minority Affairs, Government of India. It is intended for students who want to check whether they meet the requirements for financial support.

* **Scholarship Amount:** ₹25,000 to ₹30,000 per year

* **Application Deadline:** October 15, 2026
