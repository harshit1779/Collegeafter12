## CollegeAfter12 India Scholarship Information

The **Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships (CSSS)** is provided by the Ministry of Education, Govt of India.

* **Scholarship Amount:** ₹10,000 to ₹20,000 per year

* **Application Deadline:** November 30, 2026

The most useful place to start is the eligibility section. Scholarship rules can determine whether you can apply, so it is better to check those requirements before treating the funding as part of your education plan. The platform also includes application information, allowing students to move from checking the scheme to understanding how to apply.

This is especially relevant when you are comparing several courses or colleges. A scholarship amount may affect how you think about the total cost of studying, but it should be considered with the rest of your plan. Look at the course, college, exam requirements, and available support together rather than making a decision from the scholarship amount alone.
